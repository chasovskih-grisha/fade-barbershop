# Revenue & Pricing Optimization Engine

This package implements advanced revenue management and pricing optimization for the barbershop platform (`Projects/barbershop/platform`).

## Features
1. **Dynamic Peak/Off-Peak Pricing Models**: Automatically adjusts service pricing based on demand multipliers (e.g., Friday/Saturday premium, weekday morning off-peak discounts).
2. **Advanced Service & Package Bundling**: Creates optimized bundles (e.g., "The Executive Grooming Package": Haircut + Beard Trim + Hot Towel Shave + Facial Scrub) with strategic discount pricing to increase average order value (AOV).
3. **Add-on Attach Rate Optimization**: Recommends high-margin add-ons (scalp treatments, premium styling products, beard oils) during booking to maximize revenue per client.
4. **Service Tier Optimization**: Tiered pricing based on barber experience level (Master Barber, Senior Barber, Stylist) with transparent value communication.
