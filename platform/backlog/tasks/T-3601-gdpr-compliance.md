# T-3601 · GDPR Compliance

- **Stage**: M5 (phase 30) · **Tags**: backend, legal, security · **Complexity**: senior · **Depends on**: T-002, T-060
- **StoryPoints**: 18

## What
GDPR / Personal Data Protection Act: data mapping, lawful basis of processing, consent management (granular), data export (JSON/CSV), right to be forgotten (anonymization pipeline), retention policies, DPA with processors, DPIA for high-risk processing, data breach notification (within 72h), DPO contacts.

## Why
Legal requirement. Fines up to 4% of turnover. Client trust. Audit readiness.
