# T-4001 · B2B Accounts

- **Stage**: M6 (phase 40) · **Tags**: backend, marketing · **Complexity**: middle · **Depends on**: T-060, T-090, T-3300
- **StoryPoints**: 18

## What
B2B: corporate accounts (companies), employees (invitation by email/domain), centralized billing (invoicing/corporate balance), team booking management, volume discounts, administrative dashboard for company managers.

## Why
Corporate clients (e.g., local businesses partnering for employee grooming benefits) require structured multi-user accounts and invoicing rather than individual credit card payments.
