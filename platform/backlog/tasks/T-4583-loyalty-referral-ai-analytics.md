# T-4583 · Loyalty, Referral, and AI Analytics

- **Stage**: M6 (phase 15) · **Tags**: analytics, crm, ai · **Complexity**: senior · **Depends on**: T-4551, T-4554, T-4576
- **StoryPoints**: 8

## What

Advanced analytics for loyalty programs, referrals, and AI feature usage:
- Loyalty ROI analytics: cost of points/cashback vs incremental revenue, tier retention rates, redemption patterns.
- Referral funnel analytics: invite click-through rate, conversion rate, top referrers, fraud detection indicators.
- AI features usage analytics: virtual try-on adoption, recommendation click-through and conversion rates.
- Admin reporting views and automated weekly summary for founders.

## Why

Measure the effectiveness and ROI of retention and marketing programs to optimize rules and budgets.
