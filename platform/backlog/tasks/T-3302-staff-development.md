# T-3302 · Staff Development and Training Tracking

- **Stage**: M6 (phase 35) · **Tags**: hr, frontend · **Complexity**: middle · **Depends on**: T-3300, T-3301

## What

Tracking staff professional development, skill levels, internal certifications, training sessions, and performance reviews. Integration with scheduling to ensure qualified staff for specialized services.

## Why

Continuous staff development ensures high service quality and supports career progression within the network.
